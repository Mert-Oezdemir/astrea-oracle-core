# Responsible-use notice

ASTREA is currently an educational and research-focused open-source reference for consensual, low-stakes social discovery. It does not predict the future, classify dangerous people, diagnose disease, provide mental health care, detect threats, advise investments, or know the probability that a relationship will succeed.

Astronomy can compute celestial positions; that does not establish astrology as a validated method to predict personal outcomes. Any optional astrology narrative is clearly labeled **symbolic** and is excluded from compatibility scoring. The local tag-overlap score is not statistically calibrated.

## Do not use the reference engine for
- Medical or psychological diagnosis or treatment, crisis response, or access to care.
- Hiring, education admissions, housing, lending, insurance, credit, or public benefits decisions.
- Crime, security, violence, suspicious-person profiling or public safety alerts.
- Gambling, investment decisions, financial risk determination or economic forecasting.
- Covert behavior prediction, surveillance, targeted manipulation or unwanted data collection.

## Requirements for downstream developers
- Explicit, revocable consent; adult-only relationship matching; meaningful user controls.
- Independent evaluation and fairness testing of any ranking logic.
- Privacy/security review before any personal data or external AI model is used.
- Human oversight, transparent limitations, no overstatement of accuracy.
- Abuse-reporting mechanism, safety assessment, and jurisdiction-specific compliance where relevant.

For security vulnerability reports, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
